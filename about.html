Our Story

Bilum Haus PNG was founded with one vision — to empower Papua New Guinean women by sharing their handwoven bilums and crafts with the world. Each bilum is a symbol of strength, love, and tradition — woven by mothers, daughters, and grandmothers from villages across the country.

From the highlands to the coast, every design tells a story of heritage and community. When you buy a bilum, you are supporting families, preserving culture, and helping women create a sustainable income.

Our Mission

  • 🌺 Empower local PNG women artisans
  • 🌏 Promote traditional bilum weaving to the world
  • 💛 Support fair trade and cultural preservation
